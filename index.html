About

Research

Questions I’m following

How can intelligent systems reason, act, and adapt—without losing sight of practical constraints?

My interests lie at the intersection of reinforcement learning and multimodal intelligence, with a particular curiosity about visual generative models, embodied AI, and test-time optimisation. I care about both the reasoning behaviour of models and the systems questions that make those models usable: evidence, evaluation, efficiency, and adaptation under limited compute.
