About

Hi, I’m Xing Zhong, a third-year undergraduate studying Intelligent Science and Technology at Shanghai University.

I work mainly on reinforcement learning and multimodal intelligence. I’m interested in how learning systems reason and act, especially when memory and compute are limited.

I also write from time to time. This site keeps a small archive that begins with essays from my middle-school years.

01

research interests

My main research interest is reinforcement learning, particularly the post-training of language agents. I am broadly interested in how agents learn through interaction, use tools, and improve their reasoning under practical resource constraints, with related interests in multimodal and embodied intelligence.
