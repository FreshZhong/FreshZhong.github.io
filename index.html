About

Xing Zhong is a third-year undergraduate student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Shanghai University.

He is interested in reinforcement learning and multimodal intelligence, with a focus on how intelligent systems reason, act, and adapt under practical compute constraints. His current interests include:

  • Reinforcement Learning: learning and evaluation for agents that interact with tools and environments
  • Multimodal Intelligence: visual reasoning, generative models, and evidence-aware evaluation
  • Embodied AI and Efficient Inference: world models, test-time optimisation, and systems-aware acceleration

This website also keeps a small archive of personal writing. It begins with essays from his middle-school years and will grow over time.

research interests

Xing is curious about the boundary between model reasoning and system design: what evidence helps a model revise an answer, how test-time computation should be allocated, and how learning systems can remain effective when memory and compute are limited.
