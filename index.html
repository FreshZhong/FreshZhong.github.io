About

Hi, I’m Xing Zhong, a third-year undergraduate studying Intelligent Science and Technology at Shanghai University.

I work mainly on reinforcement learning and multimodal intelligence. I’m interested in how learning systems reason and act, especially when memory and compute are limited. At the moment, I’m exploring:

  • Reinforcement learning for agents that use tools and interact with environments
  • Multimodal learning, including visual reasoning, generative models, and evidence-aware evaluation
  • Efficient inference for world models and other compute-intensive generative systems

I also write from time to time. This site keeps a small archive that begins with essays from my middle-school years.

research interests

Right now I’m especially interested in three questions: when additional evidence actually helps a model revise an answer, how test-time compute should be allocated, and what makes a learning pipeline reliable on modest hardware.
